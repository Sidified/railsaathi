## 1. q024

**Answer:**

For e-tickets, you can cancel online before chart preparation [2]. The requirement of filing a Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) for e-tickets has been removed, and refunds are processed automatically upon cancellation [1][2].

reservation charts improves passenger convenience by reducing uncertainty, enabling
better planning for waitlisted travelers, and supporting passengers from remote
locations. Early charting allows alternative travel arrangements, optimizes vacant berth
utilization, and boosts transparency. These changes are aimed at removing incentives for
last-minute speculative bookings and ensuring tickets are available to genuine
passengers.
The Union Minister also announced that soon counter tickets can now be cancelled from
any railway station across the country, removing the earlier restriction of cancellation
only at the originating station. He said the requirement of filing a Ticket Deposit Receipt
(TDR) for e-tickets has been removed, and refunds will now be processed automatically
upon cancellation. In another passenger measure, passengers can now upgrade their
travel class up to 30 minutes before departure, whereas earlier changes were restricted
to before chart preparation.
He said these reforms are expected to further improve transparency, reduce misuse, and
enhance passenger experience in railway ticketing.

## 2. q080

**Question:** Agar main 48 ghante se pehle cancel karu toh full refund milna chahiye na?

**Answer:**

Aapka yeh purana rule (48 hours wala) ab replace ho chuka hai [1][2][3]. Naye rules ke mutabiq, agar aap confirmed ticket ko train ki scheduled departure se 24 aur 72 ghante ke beech mein cancel karte hain, toh fare ka 25% deduction hota hai [2]. Puri tarah se full refund 48 ghante se pehle nahi milta [2][3]. Agar aapko aur jaankari chahiye, toh aap IRCTC ya 139 se sampark kar sakte hain.

## 3. q021

**Question:** My train is late by more than 10 hr, is there any way I can cancel my train ticket for a full refund?

**Answer:**

If your train is running late by more than three hours and you choose not to travel, you can get a full refund with no cancellation charges or clerkage levied [1]. 

For e-tickets, you must file a TDR online before the actual departure of the train [1]. For i-tickets, you must surrender the ticket up to the actual departure of the train [1]. If the ticket is cancelled, surrendered, or if the refund request is filed online after the actual departure of the train, no refund of fare will be admissible [1].

## 4. q013

**Question:** I cancelled my ticket a week before the journey and still Rs 230 was deducted. Why are there cancellation charges?

**Answer:**

The old cancellation windows of 48, 12, and 4 hours were replaced in April 2026 by 72, 24, and 8 hours [1][2][3]. 

Indian Railways deducts flat minimum cancellation charges per passenger for confirmed tickets when cancelled well in advance [3]. Based on your ticket class, a minimum flat rate is deducted: Rs. 240 plus GST for AC First/Executive Class, Rs. 200 plus GST for First Class/AC 2 Tier, Rs. 180 plus GST for AC Chair Car/AC 3Tier/AC 3 Economy, Rs. 120 for Sleeper Class, and Rs. 60 for Second Class [3].
